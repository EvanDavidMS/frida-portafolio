# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, art directors and hiring leads at animation studios, design agencies and 3D/VFX houses who are screening junior talent and internship candidates. They skim fast, often on a laptop between other portfolios, sometimes on a phone from a link in an email or LinkedIn message. Their job: decide in under a minute whether Frida has real craft and a distinct voice, then dig into process for the projects that catch their eye.

## Product Purpose
The personal portfolio of Frida Delgadillo García, a Graphic Design & Animation student in Torreón, México. It shows her range (2D animation and character design, 3D modeling/lighting/shading in Maya, branding/campaigns and UX/UI) and her process (bibles, storyboards, wireframes, breakdowns). Success = a recruiter remembers her, opens at least one project in depth and reaches out.

## Brand Personality
Tierna, juguetona, talentosa. Sweet and kawaii like her characters Honey and Sage, but with visible professional craft. Voice is warm, first-person Spanish, playful without being childish. Emotional goal: delight first, then respect ("this is cute" → "wait, this is really well made").

## Anti-references
- Generic dark "creative developer" portfolios with neon gradients and glass cards.
- Template Behance/Dribbble grids where every project is the same box.
- Childish clip-art cuteness with no hierarchy, or pink used as a flat wash with nothing else going on.
- SaaS landing scaffolding: eyebrow over every section, hero metrics, icon+heading+text cards.

## Design Principles
1. The work is the hero: every decoration exists to frame her images, never to compete with them.
2. Show the process: storyboards, wireframes and breakdowns are content, not appendix.
3. Delight with intent: playful motion and stickers reward attention, but a recruiter skimming in 30 seconds still gets name, role, range and contact.
4. Her own artifacts over generic ornament: use her characters and renders as the decorative language.
5. Craft is visible in the details: typography, spacing and easing should feel as considered as her renders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast on pink surfaces. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (content is always visible without animation). Keyboard navigable gallery and filters, visible focus. Spanish (`lang="es"`).
